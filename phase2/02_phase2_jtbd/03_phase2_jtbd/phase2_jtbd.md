# Phase 2 — Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

## Core Jobs

1. **As a business leader**, I want procurement decisions to be fast and predictable, so digital and AI initiatives are not blocked.

2. **As a procurement manager**, I want clear decision rights and digital workflows, so I can reduce manual effort and delays.

3. **As an executive**, I want governance focused on outcomes and risk visibility, not tactical approvals.

4. **As a product owner (future state)**, I want authority over procurement within guardrails, so I can deliver measurable business results.

5. **As the organization**, we want procurement to enable innovation, not constrain it.
