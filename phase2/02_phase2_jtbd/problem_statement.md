# Phase 2 — Personas

## Persona 1: Procurement Manager
**Role:** Manages purchasing requests, approvals, and vendor interactions  
**Goals:**
- Ensure compliance and minimize risk
- Follow internal and regulatory procedures
**Pain Points:**
- Large volume of manual approvals
- Slow decision cycles
- No authority to improve or redesign the process
- Digital tools treated as administrative systems, not products

---

## Persona 2: Business / Operations Leader
**Role:** Requests procurement for operations, IT, or digital initiatives  
**Goals:**
- Fast execution
- Predictable timelines
**Pain Points:**
- Long procurement lead times
- Poor transparency on status
- Delays that block digital and AI initiatives

---

## Persona 3: Executive / Board Member
**Role:** Oversees risk and governance  
**Goals:**
- Avoid regulatory and financial risk
- Maintain control and accountability
**Pain Points:**
- Forced to approve operational-level decisions
- Limited visibility into real business outcomes
- Governance model does not scale with company growth
