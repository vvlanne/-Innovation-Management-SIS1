## Conclusion (Phase 1)

Air Astana demonstrates strong financial and operational performance; however, analysis of the Integrated Annual Report indicates that the organization operates primarily under a **project- and control-driven model** rather than a **product operating model**.

Digital and internal initiatives are presented as discrete implementations focused on efficiency and risk mitigation, with centralized approvals and limited evidence of product ownership, empowered teams, or outcome-based accountability. As a result, the core constraint is **not a lack of technology or AI**, but an **organizational model** that slows decision-making and inhibits the scalable impact of digital and AI initiatives.

This constraint provides the basis for Phase 2, which focuses on diagnosing and addressing a specific internal process—procurement—where risk aversion and manual controls most directly limit speed and effectiveness.
